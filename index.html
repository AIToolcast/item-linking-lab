Keep the original index

When you explicitly pair newly created outputs, the link should point to the input that produced them. After filtering or sorting, the new output position may be a different number.

n8n can also link items automatically in supported cases. This exercise starts with the displayed link metadata; it does not predict the metadata of an arbitrary workflow.

A link is not a business rule

A recorded link can itself be wrong. A single match here only means that our fictional intended customer agrees with our selected customer. It does not certify a workflow, tenant boundary or real recipient.

The exercise makes no model calls and collects no inputs or browser storage. The hosting provider may log requests or measure page visits; these are not evidence of completed tasks. Opening source links makes an ordinary request to their sites.
